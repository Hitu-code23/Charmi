The signature

Why a Russian manicure hits different.

It isn't a colour, it's a technique. It takes longer to do and the result is a set that looks neater on day one and still looks good weeks later.

CleanerColour sits close to the cuticle
LongerNeater grow-out between sets
1.

Dry prep

No soaking. Nails stay dry so the product grips better and lifts less.

2.

E-file cuticle work

Dead cuticle and skin are gently lifted and refined with precision bits for a crisp, clean frame.

3.

Shape & structure

Nails are filed to your shape and strengthened with a base or builder gel where needed.

4.

Colour, close to the cuticle

Gel goes on thin and precise, tucked right under the cuticle line for that seamless look.

5.

Finish & care

A high-shine top coat, cuticle oil and aftercare tips so your set stays perfect.

Retention

Make your set last longer.

Oil daily

Cuticle oil keeps the skin soft and the product flexible, so you get less lifting.

Gloves on

Wear gloves for dishes and cleaning. Harsh chemicals dull the shine.

Not tools

Don't use your nails to open cans or peel off stickers. Use the pads of your fingers.

Never peel

Picking off gel takes layers of your natural nail with it. Book a proper removal instead.

FAQ

Good questions.

Do I need to pay an advance?
Yes. A ₹300 advance confirms your slot and is adjusted against your final bill. It's non-refundable and non-adjustable on cancellation or a no-show. See the booking policy.
Can I walk in?
I don't take walk-ins. All services are strictly by appointment, so DM on Instagram to book your slot.
How long does a gel set last?
With good prep and aftercare, gel, BIAB and Gel-X sets usually look great for around 2–4 weeks, depending on how fast your nails grow and how hard you are on your hands.
Is a Russian manicure safe?
Yes, when it's done carefully by a trained tech. It only removes dead cuticle tissue using fine e-file bits, with no aggressive cutting into live skin.
Do you come to my place?
Yes. Alongside the home studio in Dombivli, I do home-service appointments. Travel is charged separately. DM your area and date to check availability.
Can I get a refill on a set done elsewhere?
Refills are only for eligible existing sets. Sets done elsewhere may need removal and a fresh application. Let me know about any existing extensions when you book.
